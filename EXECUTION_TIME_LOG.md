## ⏱️ Expected Timing Benchmarks

Based on typical hardware (MacBook Air M1, 8GB RAM):

| Step | Description | Approximate Time |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Step 1** | YOLO+ByteTrack Detection | 1-2 minutes |
| **Step 2** | Motion Model Building | 5 seconds |
| **Step 3** | Hybrid Cost Calculation (Motion + SSIM) | 2-3 minutes |
| **Step 4** | 2-opt Optimization | 30-60 seconds |
| **Step 5** | Video Reconstruction | 10 seconds |
| **Total** | Complete Pipeline | **4-5 minutes** |

### Breakdown by Resource Usage

**Most Time-Consuming:**
- **Step 3** (Hybrid Cost): 2-3 minutes
  - Computes 90,000 SSIM comparisons (300×300 frames)
  - Most computationally intensive step
  - Can benefit from GPU acceleration

- **Step 1** (YOLO Detection): 1-2 minutes
  - YOLOv8x inference on 300 frames
  - GPU can significantly speed this up

**Fastest Steps:**
- **Step 2** (Motion Model): ~5 seconds (simple calculations)
- **Step 5** (Video Reconstruction): ~10 seconds (file I/O)

## 🔍 Optimization Notes

**To Speed Up Execution:**

1. **GPU Acceleration**
   - YOLO detection (Step 1): 2-3x faster with GPU
   - SSIM calculation (Step 3): Can be parallelized with GPU

2. **Adjust SSIM Weight**
   - Reduce beta in Step 3 to compute fewer SSIM comparisons
   - Trade-off: Less perceptual quality optimization

3. **Hardware Upgrades**
   - More RAM: Helps with SSIM calculation
   - Faster CPU: Benefits all steps
   - SSD: Faster video I/O in Steps 1 and 5
